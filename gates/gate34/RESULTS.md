## What it means

1. **The pipeline works end to end when the owner answers.** Every link held:
   - builders recorded an assumption for each unusual rule (8/8);
   - the one-line list let the owner find all 8;
   - "what would you do instead?" captured each rule in his words;
   - one fix round put 7 of 8 into the code.
2. **So the gap in Gates 30 and 31 is the human step, not the machinery.** Gate 30's review showed only half the rules. Gate 31's founder found them (8/9) but kept every one. With an owner who answers each marked decision, the same code carries his rules in.
3. **The one miss is translation.** The owner said "keep the plan while retrying for 30 days". The builders stored the 30 days correctly, but marked the plan as "grace" instead of "same". The fix round could read an owner's sentence two ways. A follow-up idea: after the fix round, show the owner each changed behaviour as one line ("A failed renewal: the brand keeps its plan, retried for 30 days") for a final yes.
4. **What remains to show:** the same with the real founder, with the fixed answering step that Gate 31 lacked. That needs about 15 minutes of his time on a distinctive area.
