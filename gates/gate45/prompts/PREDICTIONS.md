# Gate 45 predictions, registered before any rule, sheet or case exists (Oct 29 2026)

**Question.** With a synthetic owner, the code sheet took builders from about 40% to 99.6–100% (Gates 42, 44). It is now Hive's hand-over. But those sheets were 66–68 rules, and the founder says that is too much for an owner to review; real owners audit long lists poorly (Gates 30–35: he marked 0 of 27). Hive now shows a long sheet in short pages by area (at most 8 rules, "this section is fine" per page, `7d48e80`). **Does a real owner read a paged code sheet well enough to carry his rules into the code?**

**Design.**
- **Feature:** a fresh, unbuilt area, **referrals** (creators inviting creators, brands inviting brands, rewards and their limits). It is a pure library with a fixed interface, given to every build.
- **Day 1, the sheet:**
  - N0 builds it with no owner (4 builders).
  - Hive's describer writes the code sheet from N0's code.
  - The founder reviews it on his phone through Hive's real paged sheet, measured for time, sections passed as fine, rules marked, and corrections.
  - His corrected sheet is C.
- **Day 2, the referee, at least 12 hours later:** the experimenter writes 12 situations, one per interface case, in his own framing, and the founder gives verdicts on Telegram. As before, a cold probe and a blind judge label each one surprising, ordinary or unclear, and he confirms readings of free-text verdicts. These become the hidden tests. Neither build sees them.
- **Builds:** stage 2 builds I from the goal plus C, fresh from the same base, 4 builders. N0 is the comparison.

| # | Prediction |
| --- | --- |
| P1 | **He finishes the sheet review** (every page answered) in under 15 minutes |
| P2 | He marks at least one rule wrong (Gate 35: he marked none of 27 in a flat list) |
| S1 | **On surprising hidden cases, I beats N0 by ≥ 30 percentage points** |
| S2 | Ordinary cases ≥ 75% in both builds |
| S3 | Existing backend tests pass in both builds |
| K | On the decisions both cover, his corrected sheet matches his day-2 verdict on ≥ 70% (blind judge) |

Kill: **I beats N0 by < 10 points on surprising cases.** If so, a real owner's review of a code sheet does not carry his rules, however it is paged, and the sheet should shrink to the decisions he is likely to care about, or be replaced by the "looks right?" check alone.

**Honest prior.** P1 about 70%, P2 about 75%, S1 about 40%, K about 50%. The paging makes reading lighter, but Gates 36–38 showed his answers can change with the framing, which caps S1 whatever the sheet does.

**Cost.** About $7 at list price for both builds, plus about 15 minutes of the founder on day 1 and 5 on day 2. Runs after Gate 38 is scored.
