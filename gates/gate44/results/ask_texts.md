### The owner's rules for this feature (from his interview)
The owner decided each of these himself. Encode every one that touches your area as a test before you land, follow it exactly, and do not stretch it beyond the situation it describes.

- age_band: Whenever the age is 0 up to (but not including) 18, the band is "0-17"; 18 up to 35 gives "18-34"; 35 up to 55 gives "35-54"; 55 up to 65 gives "55-64"; and 65 or more gives "65+".
- age_band: Whenever the age has a fraction, it is not rounded but compared as is, so 17.5 is "0-17", 34.9 is "18-34" and 64.99 is "55-64".
- age_band: Whenever the age is below zero (including -0.5), is NULL, is text that is not wholly a number (empty text, "abc", "40abc"), or is over 120, the result is the text "unknown", not NULL.
- age_band: Whenever the age is text holding a number, spaces around it are ignored and it is banded like the number (" 40 " gives "35-54", "1e1" gives "0-17"), but a negative number in text such as "-5" gives "unknown", not NULL.
- age_band: Whenever the age is very large, the band is "65+" up to and including an age of exactly 120. Any age over 120, including infinity, gets "unknown" rather than "65+".
- fiscal_qtr: Whenever the date is a valid ISO date YYYY-MM-DD, the result is "FY<year>-Q<n>", where the fiscal year runs 1 April to 31 March and is named after the calendar year in which it ends (2024-04-01 is "FY2025-Q1", 2024-03-31 is "FY2024-Q4").
- fiscal_qtr: Whenever the month is April-June the quarter is Q1, July-September Q2, October-December Q3, and January-March Q4.
- fiscal_qtr: Whenever the date is empty, not a real calendar date (2024-02-30, 2023-02-29, month 13, year 0000), or a number rather than text, the result is the text "N/A", not NULL. A missing (NULL) date still gives NULL.
- fiscal_qtr: Whenever the date is written in any form other than exactly four-digit year, two-digit month and two-digit day separated by hyphens (2024-5-5, 2024/05/05, or a date with a time), the result is the text "N/A", not NULL.
- fiscal_qtr: A date with spaces before or after it is not accepted; it gives the text "N/A", like any other malformed date. A real 29 February of a leap year is accepted and labelled normally (2024-02-29 is "FY2024-Q4").
- next_bday: Whenever n is a positive whole number, the result is the ISO date n business days after the given date, counting Monday to Friday only; Saturdays and Sundays are skipped, so Friday 2024-05-03 plus 1 is Monday 2024-05-06 and plus 5 is 2024-05-10.
- next_bday: Whenever the starting date is a Saturday or Sunday and n is positive, counting begins from that weekend, so the first business day is the following Monday (Saturday 2024-05-04 plus 1 is 2024-05-06).
- next_bday: Whenever n is zero, a weekday date is returned as given, but a Saturday or Sunday moves forward to the following Monday. Fractions below one in size (0.5, -0.5) also count as zero.
- next_bday: Whenever n is negative, it is handled like zero: the date does not move back. A weekday comes back as given (Monday 2024-05-06 minus 1 is still 2024-05-06), and a Saturday or Sunday moves forward to the following Monday.
- next_bday: A fractional n is cut down to its whole part (2.9 counts as 2), and numeric text such as "3" is used as that number. A negative fraction like -2.9 counts as a negative number, so it is handled like zero and does not move the date back.
- next_bday: Public holidays are not business days, but only two fixed dates count as holidays: 1 January and 25 December, in any year. Those two days are skipped, like weekends. Weekends are also non-business days. No other holidays are skipped; Easter, Thanksgiving and July 4 are ordinary business days. Examples: 2024-12-24 plus 1 is 2024-12-26, and 2024-12-31 plus 1 is 2025-01-02.
- next_bday: Whenever the date is NULL, invalid, or not in strict YYYY-MM-DD form, or n is NULL, non-numeric text ("x"), infinite, or so large that the date goes beyond the supported calendar (9999-12-31 plus 1, or n of 1,000,000,000), the result is NULL.
- next_bday: A date with spaces around it is not accepted, so the result is NULL. The result is otherwise a plain YYYY-MM-DD date that rolls over month and year ends, though 1 January and 25 December are skipped (2024-12-31 plus 1 is 2025-01-02).
- late_fee: Up to 5 days late there is no fee. From day 6 onward, the fee is 2% of the amount in cents for each started 30-day period, so days 6 to 30 are 2%, days 31 to 60 are 4%, days 61 to 90 are 6%, and so on, with no upper cap on days. The total fee is capped at 25% of the amount.
- late_fee: Whenever the fee is not a whole number of cents, it is rounded to the nearest cent with exactly half a cent rounding up (125 cents at 2% gives 3, 124 gives 2, 25 gives 1, 24 gives 0), so very small amounts can give a fee of 0.
- late_fee: Whenever days late is zero or negative, or the amount is zero or negative, the fee is 0 rather than NULL.
- late_fee: Whenever the amount or the days late is NULL, the result is NULL.
- late_fee: Whenever the amount or the days late is text that does not start with a number (empty text, "abc"), there is no fee amount and the result is NULL, not 0.
- late_fee: Whenever the amount or days late is text that is a number, that number is used, and spaces around it are ignored (" 1000 " is 1000, "10" days is 10). Text with anything else after the number, like "1000abc" or "10abc", is not a number and gives no fee amount at all, so the result is NULL.
- late_fee: Days late can have a fraction, and it is compared as is against the limits. Up to and including 5 days there is no fee, so 5.5 days is already late and gets 2%; 0.5 days counts as late and gets 2%. 30.5 days falls in the next period and gets 4%, and 60.5 days gets 6%. A fractional amount is used as is, then the fee is rounded to the nearest cent, with exactly half a cent rounding up.
- mask_card: Spaces and hyphens are removed from the card number first, and a card number given as a number is treated as its digits. A card number of 8 or more digits has every digit except the last four replaced by "*" and the last four shown, so 4111111111111111 becomes ************1111. A card number with fewer than 8 digits is not masked at all: the result is the text "INVALID".
- mask_card: Whenever the card number contains spaces or hyphens, they are removed before masking, so grouped numbers like "4111 1111 1111 1111" or "4111-1111-1111-1111" give a single unbroken mask of the same form with no separators.
- mask_card: After removing spaces and hyphens, if the card number has four or fewer digits, it is not masked. The result is the text "INVALID", the same as any number with fewer than 8 digits, so "1234", "12 34" and "123" all give "INVALID".
- mask_card: Whenever the card number is empty text or only spaces and hyphens, the result is the text "INVALID", not empty text, because it has fewer than 8 digits.
- mask_card: Whenever the card number is NULL, the result is NULL.
- mask_card: Whenever the card number contains any character other than digits, spaces and hyphens (dots, underscores, letters), the whole result is NULL. Nothing is masked and nothing is shown ("4111.1111.1111.1111" gives NULL; "abcd efgh ijkl" gives NULL).
- mask_card: Whenever the card number is given as a number rather than text, it is treated as its digits. A whole number with 8 or more digits is converted to text and masked the same way as text (12345678 gives ****5678). A whole number with fewer than 8 digits gives "INVALID". A number with a decimal fraction (123.5) is not a valid card number, so the result is NULL.
- sku_clean: When the code is exactly two letters, an optional hyphen, then exactly four digits (for example "ab-1234" or "AB1234"), the result is the letters in upper case, a hyphen, and the four digits ("AB-1234"). The code is not trimmed, and a number such as 123 is treated as text. Anything else gives the text "BAD", including three letters, fewer or more than four digits, spaces anywhere, empty text or digits only. A missing (NULL) code still gives NULL.
- sku_clean: Between the letters and the digits, only a single hyphen or nothing at all is allowed. If the letters and digits are joined by nothing at all or by a single hyphen, the result is the letters, a single hyphen, then the digits ("ab-1234" and "ab1234" both give "AB-1234"). If the separator is anything else, such as spaces, underscores, a doubled hyphen, or a hyphen with spaces around it, the result is "BAD" ("ab 1234", "ab_1234" and "ab - 1234" are each "BAD").
- sku_clean: Digits are never padded or kept longer: a code with more or fewer than four digits gives "BAD". "ABC-12345", "ab-12345", "abc-000012" and "ab-0" all give "BAD", and "ab-0000" gives "AB-0000".
- sku_clean: Whenever the code has spaces or tabs at the start or end, they are not removed; they make the code invalid, so it gives "BAD". Lower-case letters are accepted and the code is converted to upper case.
- sku_clean: Any code that is not two letters, an optional single hyphen, then exactly four digits gives the text "BAD". That covers letters only, digits only, "ab-12-3", "abc.12", "abc-12x", "abc 1 2", "abc/12", "ab-1c", accented letters, and an underscore or hyphen before the letters. Such a code is never returned trimmed or upper-cased. A missing (NULL) code still gives NULL.
- sku_clean: Whenever the code is NULL, the result is NULL; when it is empty or only spaces, the result is "BAD"; when it is a number such as 123, it is treated as text and the result is "BAD", because a number can never be valid (the code needs two letters followed by four digits).
- ship_cost: Whenever the parcel goes to zone A, B or C, the base charge is 500, 800 or 1200 cents respectively, and the base covers the first 500 g of the parcel.
- ship_cost: Whenever the parcel is heavier than 500 g, each further started 500 g beyond the first 500 g adds 100 cents (501 g to 1000 g adds 100, 1001 g to 1500 g adds 200), so 2001 g to zone B costs 1200 and 5500 g to zone C costs 2200.
- ship_cost: A weight of exactly 0 g gets no charge at all, so the result is NULL. Any weight above 0 and up to 500 g (0.5 g included) pays only the base charge.
- ship_cost: Whenever the weight is negative (including -0.5), the result is NULL.
- ship_cost: Whenever the zone is given in lower case or with spaces around it ("a", " b "), it is accepted and treated as the upper-case zone.
- ship_cost: Whenever the zone is any value other than A, B or C (D, empty text, "AB", a number), the result is the zone C rate, which is a base of 1200 cents. Only when the zone is missing (NULL) is the result NULL.
- ship_cost: Whenever the weight or the zone is NULL, the result is NULL.
- ship_cost: Weight is charged in 500 g steps. A fraction of a gram over a 500 g step boundary counts as a started 500 g and is charged as the next step: 500.5 g in zone A costs 600, and 1000.5 g in zone A costs 700. Any weight above 0 and up to 500 g, including 0.5 g, pays only the base.
- ship_cost: Whenever the weight is text that is a number ("1500", " 1500 "), that number is used, and spaces around the number are ignored ("1500" and " 1500 " both give 700 in zone A). Whenever the weight is text that is not a number ("abc", empty text), there is no charge at all and the result is NULL, not the base. Text with anything else after the number ("1500abc") is also not a number and gives NULL.
- ship_cost: Any parcel heavier than 30,000 g is charged a flat 9,999 cents, whatever the zone, so 1,000,000 g to zone A costs 9,999 cents. A parcel of exactly 30,000 g is still charged by the normal weight steps.
- split_bill: Whenever the total in whole cents is split among people, each pays the total divided by the number of people rounded up to a whole cent, so the bill is always covered or over-covered (1000 among 3 is 334, 999 among 3 is 333, 1 among 2 is 1).
- split_bill: Whenever the number of people is less than 1 (zero, negative, or a fraction below 1 like 0.5), the number of people is treated as one person, so the one person pays the whole total (1000 among 0 people is 1000). The result is NULL only when the total is NULL, or the number of people is NULL, or the number of people is text that isn't wholly a number (empty text, "abc", "3abc"). Spaces around numeric text are ignored.
- split_bill: Whenever the total is zero the amount each pays is 0, and when the total is negative the result is the share rounded toward zero (-1000 among 3 and -1001 among 3 both give -333).
- split_bill: Whenever the total is a whole number and the number of people has a fraction, the people count is cut down to its whole part (1000 among 2.5 people is split as 2 people, 500; 1000 among 3.9 is split as 3 people, 334; 1000 among 1.5 is 1000).
- split_bill: Whenever the total has a fraction or is given as a decimal, it is divided by the people count, which is cut down to its whole part just like a whole-number total (and fewer than one person counts as one). The share is then rounded up to a whole cent. A negative total is rounded toward zero. Examples: 1000.5 among 2 gives 501, 1000.5 among 2.5 people is split as 2 and gives 501, 1000.5 among 3.9 is split as 3 and gives 334, and -1000.5 among 3 gives -333.
- split_bill: Whenever the total is text that is wholly a number ("1000", with spaces around it ignored), that number is used. Whenever the total is text that is not wholly a number (empty text, "abc", "1000abc"), the result is NULL, not 0.
- star_rating: Whenever there are 10 or more votes, the result is the average score rounded to the nearest half star, with exactly halfway values rounding up (4.2 gives 4.0, 4.25 gives 4.5, 4.24 gives 4.0, 4.75 gives 5.0, 2.74 gives 2.5, 2.75 gives 3.0).
- star_rating: Whenever there are fewer than 5 votes (4, 4.9, zero, negative), the result is NULL, however good the average; exactly 5 votes is enough, and 5.0 is also enough.
- star_rating: Whenever the average score is above 5 the rating is 5.0, and when it is below 0 the rating is 0.0; the rating is always a number from 0 to 5 in steps of 0.5.
- star_rating: Whenever the average score or the votes is NULL, the result is NULL.
- star_rating: Whenever the votes is text with no number (empty text, "abc"), it counts as 0 votes and the result is NULL, and numeric text such as "50" is used as that number.
- star_rating: Whenever the average score is text that isn't wholly a number (empty text, "abc", "4abc"), it gives no rating at all, so the result is NULL, not 0.0. Numeric text such as "4.5" is used as that number, and spaces around it are ignored. The rating applies given enough votes.
- trim_avg: Whenever there are three or more scores in the group, the single lowest and the single highest score are dropped (only one of each, even if several are tied) and the remaining scores are averaged, so 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 gives 3.0.
- trim_avg: Whenever there are fewer than three scores, there is no result at all: with exactly two scores, or just one, the result is NULL, and nothing is averaged.
- trim_avg: Whenever a score is NULL, it is ignored entirely and does not count towards the three needed for trimming.
- trim_avg: Whenever there are no scores at all (an empty table, no matching rows, or only NULLs), the result is NULL.
- trim_avg: Whenever a score is text, including numeric text such as "4" and text with no number (empty text, "x"), it is ignored entirely, the same as NULL. Text scores do not count towards the three scores needed, so "x" is never counted as a 0 and "4" is never counted as a 4.
- trim_avg: Whenever scores are grouped (GROUP BY), the trimmed average is worked out separately for each group, and the result is a decimal number (3.0, 2.714...) without rounding.
- - late_fee: An invoice with an amount of zero or below owes no fee, however late it is.
- ship_cost: The zone is trimmed of spaces and is not case-sensitive, so " b " and "a" count as zones B and A.
- ship_cost: A negative weight gets no charge at all, so the result is NULL, the same as a weight of 0 g.
- next_bday: An n above 100,000 business days, or a result past the last date the calendar can show, gives NULL.

- split_bill: Whenever the total has a fraction or is given as a decimal, it is divided by the people count, which is cut down to its whole part just like a whole-number total (and fewer than one person counts as one). The share is then rounded up to a whole cent. A negative total is rounded toward zero. Examples: 1000.5 among 2 gives 501, 1000.5 among 2.5 people is split as 2 and gives 501, 1000.5 among 3.9 is split as 3 and gives 334, and -1000.5 among 3 gives -333.

- star_rating: Whenever there are 10 or more votes, the result is the average score rounded to the nearest half star, with exactly halfway values rounding up (4.2 gives 4.0, 4.25 gives 4.5, 4.24 gives 4.0, 4.75 gives 5.0, 2.74 gives 2.5, 2.75 gives 3.0).
- star_rating: Whenever there are fewer than 5 votes (4, 4.9, zero, negative), the result is NULL, however good the average; exactly 5 votes is enough, and 5.0 is also enough.
- star_rating: Whenever the average score is above 5 the rating is 5.0, and when it is below 0 the rating is 0.0; the rating is always a number from 0 to 5 in steps of 0.5.
- star_rating: Whenever the average score or the votes is NULL, the result is NULL.
- star_rating: Whenever the votes is text with no number (empty text, "abc"), it counts as 0 votes and the result is NULL, and numeric text such as "50" is used as that number.
- star_rating: Whenever the average score is text that isn't wholly a number (empty text, "abc", "4abc"), it gives no rating at all, so the result is NULL, not 0.0. Numeric text such as "4.5" is used as that number, and spaces around it are ignored. The rating applies given enough votes.
- trim_avg: Whenever there are three or more scores in the group, the single lowest and the single highest score are dropped (only one of each, even if several are tied) and the remaining scores are averaged, so 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 gives 3.0.
- trim_avg: Whenever there are fewer than three scores, there is no result at all: with exactly two scores, or just one, the result is NULL, and nothing is averaged.
- trim_avg: Whenever a score is NULL, it is ignored entirely and does not count towards the three needed for trimming.
- trim_avg: Whenever there are no scores at all (an empty table, no matching rows, or only NULLs), the result is NULL.
- trim_avg: Whenever a score is text, including numeric text such as "4" and text with no number (empty text, "x"), it is ignored entirely, the same as NULL. Text scores do not count towards the three scores needed, so "x" is never counted as a 0 and "4" is never counted as a 4.
- trim_avg: Whenever scores are grouped (GROUP BY), the trimmed average is worked out separately for each group, and the result is a decimal number (3.0, 2.714...) without rounding.
