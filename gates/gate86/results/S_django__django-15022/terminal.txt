  3. Client search: a client is left out if even one word is found nowhere. "Sharma Zzz" returns no clients, even though Sharma Textiles matches "Sharma".
  4. Client search: the order of the words does not matter. "Textiles Sharma" finds the same client as "Sharma Textiles".
  5. Client search: a word matches when it appears anywhere inside a detail, not only at the start. "pune" finds the event "Pune show", and "Textiles" finds "Sharma Textiles".
  6. Client search: the same word typed twice acts like one word. "Sharma Sharma" lists both Sharma Textiles and Sharma Other.
  7. Client search: when the words are found in several of a client's events or members, the client is still listed once. Sharma Textiles has two events matching "Pune" and appears one time.
  8. Client search: words wrapped in double quotes are treated as one phrase. "\"Sharma Textiles\" pune" finds Sharma Textiles, and "\"pune show\" asha" does too.
  9. Client search: single quotes also make a phrase. Typing 'Pune show' with single quotes finds Sharma Textiles.
  10. Client search: an opening quote that is never closed is taken as part of the word. Typing "Sharma Textiles with only the first quote finds nobody, because that text with the quote mark is not found anywhere.
  11. Client search: a detail marked as starting-with only matches words at the beginning. With that setting, "sharma" finds Sharma Other and Sharma Textiles, but "textiles" finds nobody.
  12. Client search: a detail marked as exact only matches a word that is the whole detail. "sharma textiles" finds nobody, since it is split into two words and neither alone equals the full name.
  13. Client search: a detail can be the client's reference number. Searching the number of Sharma Textiles finds it, and a word like "x" finds nobody.
  14. Client search: if no details are set up for searching, typing words changes nothing and every client is listed. Typing "pune" still shows Sharma Other and Sharma Textiles.
  15. Messy or missing details: capital letters are ignored. "sharma  PUNE" and "SHARMA   rao  " find Sharma Textiles just as lowercase words would.
  16. Messy or missing details: extra spaces between words or around them are ignored. "sharma   PUNE" works the same as "sharma pune".
  17. Messy or missing details: an empty search shows all clients. Leaving the box empty lists both Sharma Other and Sharma Textiles.
  18. Messy or missing details: a word that matches nothing returns an empty list instead of an error. "Sharma Zzz" shows no clients.
