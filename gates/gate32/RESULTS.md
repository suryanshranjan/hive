# Gate 32 results: not run (too few surprising cases), Oct 28 2026

Only aggregate numbers are published. The company's situations and the founder's verdicts stay private.

**What happened.**
1. The founder gave verdicts on Telegram for 12 situations about campaign selection and invitations.
2. A cold default probe (3 runs, unanimous on 9 of 12) answered the same situations.
3. The blind judge (3 votes, not told whose answer was whose) labelled **5 surprising, 7 ordinary, 0 unclear.**

The design inherited from Gate 30 requires at least 6 surprising cases, or the gate is not run. **So Gate 32 was not run.** No build was made, so no prediction is scored.

**Finding: how surprising an owner is depends on the area.**

| Gate | Area | Surprising | Ordinary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 30 | The deal's journey | 6 | 4 (and 2 unclear) |
| 31 | AI credits | 9 | 3 |
| 32 | Campaign selection and invitations | 5 | 7 |

Where the founder's rules are close to common practice, as in selection and invitations, a builder's default already gets most of them right. Where the business is new or its own, as with credits, plans and what is charged, most of his rules differ. **For Hive this says where an owner's review is worth the most: the parts of the product that are distinctive.** A future version could weigh the review toward those parts (model).

**Next.** Test the fixed answering step (Gate 31's lesson) in an area with enough surprising rules, such as more of credits and plans, or payments.
