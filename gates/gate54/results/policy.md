# Tiny Shop policies (the owner's own rules; never shown to the builders)

## Refunds
1. A refund is worked out on what the customer actually paid for the items: item prices times quantities, minus the discount code.
2. Within 14 days of delivery (day 14 included): the full amount. From day 15 to day 30: half. After day 30: nothing.
3. Damaged goods (reason "damaged"): always the full amount, up to 90 days after delivery; after 90 days, nothing.
4. The shipping fee is never refunded, except for damaged goods, where it is refunded too.
5. Final-sale orders get no refund, unless the goods arrived damaged.
6. Refunds under 50 rupees are not paid (the amount is 0), except for damaged goods.
7. Amounts are rounded down to whole rupees.
8. The money goes back the way it was paid: store credit orders as store credit, card orders to the card.
9. A negative number of days (a mistake in the data) is treated as day 0.

## Loyalty points
10. One point for every full 100 rupees paid for the items (after the discount code); shipping earns nothing.
11. Orders paid with store credit earn no points.
12. Orders where the items come to less than 500 rupees (after the discount) earn no points.
13. Final-sale orders earn double points.

## Sales summary (for the owner's daily look)
14. "Orders" counts every order in the list, including store-credit ones.
15. "Revenue" is the money that came in: the totals (items after discount, plus shipping) of card orders only. Store-credit orders bring in no new money.
16. "Average" is revenue divided by the number of card orders, rounded down to whole rupees; 0 if there are no card orders.
